LAYER 01

Physical Catchment & IoT Edge Sensing

Solar-powered telemetry pods equipped with ESP32-S3 microcontrollers and JSN-SR04T IP68 ultrasonic transducers. Each unit executes on-chip Kalman filtering to reject surface turbulent ripples before transmission.

LAYER 02

Telemetry Transport & Gateway Ingestion

Dual-path LoRaWAN (868/915 MHz) and 4G-LTE cellular backhaul with TLS 1.3 encrypted MQTT brokers. Automated failover ensures uninterrupted data ingestion even during cellular power outages.

LAYER 03

FLUXORA Forecast Core & Predictive Analytics

Hybrid hydrodynamic models combining Saint-Venant 1D shallow water channel equations with real-time Manning roughness coefficient adaptation for silt and debris accumulation.

LAYER 04

Emergency Response & SCADA Mitigation Actuation

Closed-loop Modbus TCP/IP integration with municipal drainage pumps and motorized sluice gates. Automated Common Alerting Protocol (CAP v1.2) emergency broadcasts sent to NDMA and municipal disaster helplines.
